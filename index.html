Commercial Investment Mortgage in London in 2026: Interest Cover and the 700,000 Pound Lot

Ninety fewer buildings sold for 5 million pounds or more in London last year. The count went from 530 in 2024 to 440 in 2025, a fall of 17 percent, while the price of the typical building barely shifted, from 730,000 pounds to 700,000 pounds. Those two facts say who was still buying. The institutions and funds that trade large lots stepped back. The private landlord buying one let building, a shop, a small office, a light industrial unit with a single tenant, kept going. A commercial investment mortgage is how that landlord pays for it, and the test that decides the loan is not the one most buyers prepare for. Loan to value gets the attention. Interest cover does the deciding. This piece runs the cover sum in full on a 700,000 pound building, the London median, and shows where in the capital these purchases are most often financed.

The cover sum on a 700,000 pound building

The interest cover ratio, or ICR, is the yearly rent divided by the yearly interest, and lenders work out that interest at a stressed rate set above the rate the borrower will pay. The indicative requirement on our lender panel is 140 to 160 percent.

The figures here are illustrative. The building costs 700,000 pounds, the 2025 London median in HM Land Registry data. We have used a stress rate of 8.5 percent, which is the top of the investment rate band and 200 basis points above the bottom of it. Each lender sets its own stress rate, so treat this as one reasonable case, not a rule.

Step one, the loan the valuation allows. At the 75 percent ceiling the loan is 525,000 pounds and the deposit is 175,000 pounds.

Step two, the stressed interest. 525,000 pounds at 8.5 percent is 44,625 pounds a year.

Step three, the rent that loan needs. At 140 percent cover the building must produce 44,625 times 1.4, which is 62,475 pounds a year. At 160 percent it must produce 71,400 pounds.

Step four, the reality check. 62,475 pounds is about 8.9 percent of the purchase price. 71,400 pounds is 10.2 percent. A building let at less than that will not carry a 75 percent loan on this stress, whatever the valuer says it is worth.

Now run it the other way, from a rent. Say the building lets at 49,000 pounds a year, which is 7 percent of the price. Divide by 1.4 and the lender will accept stressed interest of 35,000 pounds. Divide that by 8.5 percent and the largest loan is about 411,800 pounds, or 58.8 percent of the price. The deposit is no longer 175,000 pounds. It is about 288,200 pounds.

On a let building the valuer tells you the most a lender could advance. The rent tells you what it will.

The lease does as much work as the rent

Cover tells a lender whether the rent pays the interest. The lease tells it how long that rent can be relied on. On an investment case, unexpired term is the driver borrowers most often overlook.

Take two buildings with the same rent. One has ten years left on its main lease and a tenant with filed accounts. The other has two years left and a break clause in eighteen months. The first sits near the bottom of the 6.5 to 8.5 percent band. The second prices near the top, at a lower loan to value, if a term lender will quote on it at all.

The rent the underwriter uses is also his own version of it. Expect him to ignore rent from a tenant in arrears, discount rent under a lease with a break inside the fixed period, strip out any turnover element and allow for voids where a building has several tenants. The adjusted rent is nearly always lower than the rent on the agent’s particulars, and it is the one that goes into the sum above.

The 28 percent is a floor. Individuals, partnerships, pension schemes and most overseas buyers do not file charges, so the true share bought with debt is higher. A charge also records that security was given, not how much was lent, which is why these are counts and not lending volumes.

The contrast is outer London against the centre. In Enfield, Croydon and Harrow more than a third of sales were bought by a company with borrowing. In Westminster and the City it was under a fifth, because prime central buildings are more often bought with cash, by overseas buyers or through structures that file no UK charge.

Where we could identify the lender on those 1,133 purchases, specialist and challenger banks funded 304, bridging lenders 257 and high-street banks 202. Private banks, smaller building societies and other lenders accounted for 362. Roughly one financed purchase in four started on a bridge, and every one of those borrowers will need a term mortgage to repay it if the building is being kept.

Outlook

The Bank of England raised Bank Rate from 3.75 percent to 4 percent at the meeting ending 4 November 2026, and the next decision is due on 17 December 2026. On an investment loan a higher base rate lifts the stress rate as well as the pay rate, so the largest loan a given rent supports gets smaller. The usual five-year fix inside a 15 to 25 year term settles the pay rate for the fixed period but does nothing for the cover test on the day of application.

Companies House filings show 20,817 company charges on London property between January and September 2026, up 18.9 percent on the same months of 2025. Those charges cover all property that companies borrow against, most of it residential investment, so they measure lender activity, not commercial lending alone.

Early 2026 sales point the same way as 2025 on price. Land Registry had registered 1,245 sales for the year when the data was cut, at a median of 675,000 pounds. That count is partial and cannot be set against a full year.

FAQ

What interest cover do lenders want on a commercial investment mortgage? The indicative mid-2026 requirement is 140 to 160 percent, worked out on interest at a stressed rate above the pay rate. A strong tenant on a long lease tends to sit at the lower figure and short or multi-let income at the higher.

What rate will I pay on a commercial investment mortgage in London? The indicative band is 6.5 to 8.5 percent a year at 65 to 75 percent loan to value. Tenant strength, unexpired lease term and leverage decide where in the band a case lands, and the step from 65 to 75 percent loan to value is usually worth 25 to 75 basis points.

Why is my loan lower than 75 percent of the valuation? Because two caps apply and the lower one wins. If the rent does not cover the stressed interest by the lender’s required margin, the loan is cut until it does, whatever the valuation says.

Is this the same as a buy-to-let mortgage? No. Buy-to-let covers houses and flats let to residential tenants. Commercial investment lending is secured on property let to businesses and is judged on the lease, the tenant and the cover.
